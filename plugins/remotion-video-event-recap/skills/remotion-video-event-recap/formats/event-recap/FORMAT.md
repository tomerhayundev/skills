# Event recap

Status: stub (nearest: promo). Read [promo](../promo/FORMAT.md), then apply the deltas below.

## Fits when

After an event: "recap", "highlights", "conference", "meetup". The material is footage and photos, not product UI.

## Lengths and platforms

60 s default, 30-90 s; a 15 s cutdown. LinkedIn, Instagram, YouTube.

## Story shape

The moment that best shows the energy first · 3 or 4 moments (arrival, the talk, the people, the peak) · the numbers (attendees, talks: real) · next event or thank you.

## Assets and voice

Footage and photos with written permission from recognisable people. Music with a clear lift for the peak moment.

## Engine profile

The promo profile, except continuity: hard cuts on the beat between footage clips are allowed and declared in cuts.json; the reason: real footage cuts, and on the beat it reads as edited, not broken.

## Build notes

Cut footage on the beat grid; photos move with the camera's breath and ease, never a slideshow of static frames. Motif transitions between sections.

## Verification

Pops with --cuts (only beat cuts declared). Faces and names cleared.

## Common mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| Every photo from the event | 3 or 4 moments, the best of each |
