# Onboarding

Status: stub (nearest: tutorial). Read [tutorial](../tutorial/FORMAT.md), then apply the deltas below.

## Fits when

"onboarding", "welcome video", "getting started", "first steps". A series of short tutorials, one task each.

## Lengths and platforms

60 s per task, 30-120 s. Played inside the product or the help center; often muted, so caption-led works well.

## Story shape

What you will be able to do (3 s) · the task in 1-3 steps · where to go next (the next video in the series).

## Assets and voice

The product's real UI; a consistent series look (the same motif and step markers in every video).

## Engine profile

The tutorial profile unchanged.

## Build notes

Build the series from one template: each video is the tutorial module with its own task.

## Verification

The tutorial checks, per video; the series reads as one set on a contact sheet.

## Common mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| One long onboarding video | One task per video |
