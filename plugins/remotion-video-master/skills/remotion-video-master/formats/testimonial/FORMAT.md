# Testimonial

Status: stub (nearest: explainer). Read [explainer](../explainer/FORMAT.md), then apply the deltas below.

## Fits when

"testimonial", "case study", "customer story", "review video". The material is a real customer's words.

## Lengths and platforms

60 s default, 30-90 s; a 2-3 min full version; a 15 s quote cutdown.

## Story shape

The customer's problem in their words · what changed · the result (a real number) · the customer's name, role and company.

## Assets and voice

A real quote or interview with the customer's written consent to use their name, face and words; no invented or edited-to-change-meaning quotes. Subtitles for the interview.

## Engine profile

The explainer profile, except continuity: cuts between interview takes are allowed on sentence ends and declared; the reason: an interview is edited speech.

## Build notes

Cut interview footage on sentence ends; a lower-third with the name; the motif carries the before and after.

## Verification

Pops with --cuts at declared sentence-end cuts; consent on file; the quote matches the source.

## Common mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| Paraphrasing the customer into marketing copy | Their words, cut, never rewritten |
