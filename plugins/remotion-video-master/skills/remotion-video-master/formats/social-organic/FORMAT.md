# Social clip

Status: stub (nearest: promo). Read [promo](../promo/FORMAT.md), then apply the deltas below.

## Fits when

Organic posts: "post", "Reel", "TikTok", "clip", "for the feed". A paid placement is the promo's short ad.

## Lengths and platforms

30 s default, 15-90 s; Reels engagement peaks at 45-60 s and reach drops past 3 min. 9:16, inside the platform's safe zone.

## Story shape

The hook in the first second (the feed decides by then) · one idea told as a small story · a loopable end or a question that invites comments.

## Assets and voice

Sound matters: TikTok is sound-on; add burned-in captions anyway (many watch muted). Trending audio is the user's choice and license, not the skill's.

## Engine profile

The promo profile, except captions may be word-by-word pages (`createTikTokStyleCaptions`) when voiced; the reason: native feed style.

## Build notes

Design for the loop: the last frame leads into the first. Keep text inside the safe zone of every platform it is posted to.

## Verification

The promo checks plus the seam scan when it loops, and the safe zone of each platform.

## Common mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| A landscape video letterboxed into 9:16 | Reframe with the camera windows |
