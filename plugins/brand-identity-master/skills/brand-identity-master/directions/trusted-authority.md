# Trusted authority

Steady, clear, accountable. The board reads like a document you can rely on.

## When it fits

- Authority 4 to 5 on the axes.
- Steady, symmetric marks, shields, seals, monograms, strong serifs or upright sans.
- A deep primary: navy, bottle green, burgundy, slate.
- Often law, finance, health, education, public and advisory services.

## Mood words

steady, clear, accountable, calm, careful.

## Avoid

trendy, playful, flashy, vague, loud.

## Type

- Pairings: `auth-baskerville-source`, `auth-garamond-lato`, `auth-archivo-public`, `auth-playfair-franklin`, `tech-plex`, `lux-cinzel-josefin`. Hebrew: `auth-he-frank-heebo`, `auth-he-david-assistant`.
- Body 18 px for easy reading.
- A strong, regular hierarchy (display, section head, body, label); tabular numbers.
- Headings in sentence case.
- Labels may be small capitals with light tracking (not for Hebrew).

## Colour

- The deep primary from the logo, on a light ground. Body text reaches 7:1 or more.
- Accents are restrained: one, used for links or one highlight. When the logo holds one hue, a deep foil from `support.foils` may carry one band.
- Neutrals are cool or neutral, following the primary.
- A dark ground is used only for one band (the reversed logo).

## Layout

### A Report

Two zones: a narrow label sidebar (3 spans) and the content (9 spans) set in two or three columns, like a well-set annual report. Section heads are aligned in the sidebar.

### B Charter

The mark centred at the top on generous space, the name beneath it. Below, ruled sections in two columns with hairline rules between them.

### C Ledger

Specifications set as ruled tables side by side: colours, type sizes and logo rules in rows with labels and values. The logo system sits above in a calm 4-up row.

In every variant, alignment is strict, rules are hairline, and nothing is tilted.

## Pattern

`proportion-grid` at a very low strength, behind the hero or a band; `stroke-lines` as fine horizontal rules. Patterns stay in the background and never carry meaning.

## Mockups first

`letterhead`, `envelope`, `business-card`, `website-home`, `wayfinding-sign`, `invoice`. Paper and the website lead. An `invoice` shows text bars, never amounts.

## The one bold move

Choose one:

- a full-width band of the primary carrying the reversed mark, large;
- a large ruled specimen of the display face, set like an engraving;
- the seal or mark at hero size with a hairline frame.

## Bans

- Script and handwriting faces; rounded bubbly type.
- Gradients; tilted mockups; stock photos of handshakes and columns.
- Claims of trust, age or standing the brand does not make ("trusted since", "leading").
- Decorative section numbering (01, 02, 03).

## Checked against

- Quiet luxury: shares serifs. Luxury is darker, sparer and metallic; authority is lighter, denser and regular. Luxury's single-line hero is not used here.
- Precise tech: shares order. Tech's mono labels and bright action colour are out; authority's figures are tabular but set in the text face.
- Warm handmade: handmade's kraft, tilt and drawn accents are out.
- Bright playful: the opposite energy. No saturation, no rounded type, no tilt.
- Natural organic: organic's flowing bands and earth palette are out; authority stays upright and ruled.
