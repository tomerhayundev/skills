# Natural organic

Calm, grounded, fresh. The board breathes like a morning outside.

## When it fits

- Warmth 3 to 5 with energy 1 to 3 on the axes.
- Leaves, waves, sun, flowing lines, soft organic shapes.
- Earthy greens, sands, clays, sky blues; low saturation.
- Often wellness, food producers, outdoor and garden, natural cosmetics, farms.

## Mood words

calm, natural, grounded, fresh, honest.

## Avoid

synthetic, glossy, loud, techy, chrome.

## Type

- Pairings: `nat-fraunces-work`, `nat-lora-karla`, `nat-dmserif-dmsans`, `nat-garamond-raleway`, `lux-gloock-instrument`, `warm-young-karla`. Hebrew: `nat-he-frank-rubik`.
- A serif display with soft terminals, set at regular weight.
- A humanist sans for text at 17 to 18 px, with generous line height.
- Labels in small capitals with light tracking, or sentence case.

## Colour

- Earth and plant tones from the logo; neutrals of sand, stone and oat. When the logo holds one hue, a second earth or plant tone from the near foils in `support.foils`.
- Low-chroma tints for grounds.
- A light ground; a deep green or clay band may carry the reversed logo once.
- Never pure white, never pure black.

## Layout

### A Field notes

Generous outer margins, and rows of two blocks set like a notebook spread. Thin pattern bands (`stroke-lines`, wide gaps) separate the rows like pages.

### B Botanical plate

The mark whole and centred on a light ground, the name beneath, with a large `mark-crop` in a soft tint at one side. The system blocks follow in rows of two or three, like the captions of a plate.

### C Horizon

Wide bands stepping through the palette from light to deep, like layers of a landscape, each band holding two or three blocks side by side.

In every variant, curves come from the logo, corners are soft (8 to 16 px), and blocks breathe inside without empty bands between them.

## Pattern

`mark-crop` (a soft, oversize crop of the mark), `stroke-lines` with wide gaps, light `detail-dots` like seeds. Patterns are pale and few.

## Mockups first

`product-label`, `shopping-bag`, `product-box`, `social-post`, `website-home`, `sticker`. Packaging leads, with natural paper colours.

## The one bold move

Choose one:

- the mark large and whole on a band of the primary, with the pattern grown from it around it;
- a single large word of the brand's own in the display face;
- the horizon bands themselves, at full width.

## Bans

- Neon; chrome and metallic effects; hard black.
- Stock leaf, drop or globe icons that are not from the logo.
- "Eco", "natural", "green" or "sustainable" said about the brand unless the brand says it.
- High-saturation accents.

## Checked against

- Quiet luxury: shares the calm. Luxury's dark grounds and metal are out; organic stays light and earthy.
- Precise tech: tech's grids, mono type and bright action colour are out.
- Warm handmade: shares earth tones. Handmade is about people and paper, organic about plants and air; handmade's kraft grain and tilt are not used here.
- Trusted authority: authority's ruled tables and institutional deep blues are out.
- Bright playful: playful's saturation, heavy type and tilt are out.
