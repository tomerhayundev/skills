# Bright playful

Bright, friendly, full of energy. The board is the one you smile at.

## When it fits

- Energy 4 to 5 on the axes.
- Round, bold, bouncy marks; mascots; thick strokes.
- Saturated colours, often two or three.
- Often children, snacks and drinks, apps, events, fitness, community projects.

## Mood words

bright, friendly, bold, upbeat, inviting.

## Avoid

muted, stiff, formal, gloomy, fussy.

## Type

- Pairings: `play-fredoka-nunito`, `play-baloo-quicksand`, `play-bricolage-outfit`, `play-rubik`, `warm-caveat-cabin`. Hebrew: `play-he-fredoka-rubik`, `play-he-secular-heebo`.
- Heavy display weights are welcome (600 to 800).
- Rounded sans for text at 17 to 18 px.
- Labels may be bold rather than tracked.

## Colour

- The logo's colours at full strength on a light ground.
- Up to two accents, each with a job: one for action, one for highlights.
- Text on yellow, lime or light orange is always dark. `palette.mjs` sets the `on` colour; trust it.
- Colour fields are big and flat: no gradients unless the logo has one.

## Layout

### A Sticker sheet

Mockups and logo tiles tilted by up to 4 degrees, scattered on large colour fields, as if stuck down by hand. The grid underneath stays straight, and no text is tilted.

### B Poster stack

Full-bleed colour bands, one per block, alternating the logo's colours. Each band carries one idea at a big size.

### C Toy box

Modules with a 24 px or larger radius on a light ground, each module a different brand colour, arranged like blocks in a box.

In every variant:

- shapes are round;
- spacing is generous but energetic (gaps of 48 to 64 px);
- the logo appears large at least twice.

## Pattern

`detail-dots` bold and bright, `mark-tile` in two brand colours, thick `stroke-lines`. Patterns can be loud here, but never behind small text.

## Mockups first

`social-post`, `sticker`, `app-screen`, `shopping-bag`, `product-box`, `profile-picture`. Social and packaging lead.

## The one bold move

Choose one:

- the mark huge and cropped in a full-bleed primary band;
- a wall of `mark-tile` in two brand colours;
- the brand name in the display face at full board width.

## Bans

- Hairline serifs; grey on grey; text under 14 px.
- More than three colours inside one module.
- Tilted text; emoji used as decoration.
- Muddy tints of the brand colours.

## Checked against

- Quiet luxury: the opposite. Luxury's emptiness, metal and hairlines are out.
- Precise tech: tech's level grid, mono type and single action colour are too strict here, but the spacing rhythm still holds.
- Warm handmade: shares friendliness. Playful is brighter and bolder; handmade's kraft and earth tones are out.
- Trusted authority: authority's ruled tables and deep colours are out.
- Natural organic: organic's calm earth palette and low-chroma tints are out.
