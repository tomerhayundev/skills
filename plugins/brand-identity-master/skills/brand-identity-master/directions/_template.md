# Direction name

For maintainers adding a direction. Copy this file and keep every heading in this order. `directions.test.mjs` checks each direction file (not this one) for these headings, between 50 and 130 lines, at least three of its own pairings, two recipes and four deliverables in backticks, and a line for every other direction under "Checked against". Add the direction to `data/directions.json` and give it pairings in `data/fonts.json`.

## When it fits

The axis scores, the kinds of marks and colours, and the usual sectors. The sector never decides alone.

## Mood words

Five to seven words the board should feel like.

## Avoid

Five words it must not feel like.

## Type

Its pairing ids in backticks (four Latin at least, one Hebrew at least), then the rules for display, labels and body size.

## Colour

Where the ground comes from, how many accents, how neutrals are tinted, what gradients are allowed.

## Layout

### A First variant

Its structure in two or three sentences: spans, order, what the hero does.

### B Second variant

The same.

### C Third variant

The same, then the spacing rules shared by all three.

## Pattern

The recipe ids in backticks that suit it, and how strong they may be.

## Mockups first

Deliverable ids in backticks, in the order to show them.

## The one bold move

Two or three choices, each big enough to be seen from across a room.

## Bans

What this direction never does.

## Checked against

One line per other direction: how this file's rules differ, so no rule leaks across.
