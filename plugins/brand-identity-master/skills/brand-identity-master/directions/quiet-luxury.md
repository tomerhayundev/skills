# Quiet luxury

Hushed, rare, considered. The board says less and holds more space than any other direction.

## When it fits

- Luxury 4 to 5 and energy 1 to 2 on the axes.
- Serif capitals, monograms, fine rings and hairlines, a crest kept simple.
- Metal (gold, brass, silver) or one deep colour, on a dark or bone ground.
- Often hospitality, fashion, beauty, fine goods, private services. The sector alone never decides: a bakery with a gold monogram may belong here, a hotel with a cartoon mark does not.

## Mood words

quiet, restrained, warm metal, unhurried, considered, intimate, precise.

## Avoid

loud, glossy, busy, discount, cute, techy.

## Type

- Pairings: `lux-cormorant-montserrat`, `lux-bodoni-jost`, `lux-cinzel-josefin`, `lux-italiana-work`, `lux-gloock-instrument`. Hebrew: `lux-he-frank-assistant`.
- Display is set large and light. Never bold; at most the regular weight of a high-contrast face.
- Labels are uppercase with 0.16 to 0.24em tracking (never for Hebrew), at 13 px.
- Body at most 17 px, generous line height (1.6).
- One italic accent is allowed (a single word in the hero line), never more.

## Colour

- The ground is the darkest neutral or the light neutral (bone, ivory), never a mid-grey.
- The logo's metal or colour is the only accent and covers at most a tenth of the board.
- Tints stay close to their base. No bright steps from the scale on the board itself; show them in the strip only.
- Neutrals are warm when the logo is warm (gold, brass), cool when it is cool (silver, ink blue).
- Gradients only as a whisper on the hero ground (a few percent of lightness), or none.

## Layout

### A Gallery column

A tall full-width hero, then every block in a centred 8-span column with wide margins. Mockups sit in a row of three below. It feels like walking through a quiet gallery.

### B Split folio

The hero takes 7 spans and the essence sits beside it in 5. Then rows alternate 7/5 and 5/7, each with one block. Captions sit under the blocks like folio notes.

### C Monogram grid

A square hero with the mark centred inside `stroke-rings`. Then equal tiles four across for the logo system and the palette, as if laid out on a jeweller's tray.

In every variant:

- generous space between cards and sections (96 px between sections);
- every card full and finished: quiet means few colours and fine lines, never empty tiles;
- body copy never spans more than 8 columns.

## Pattern

`stroke-rings` for round marks, `mark-crop` for marks with a strong curve or serif, fine `stroke-lines` for straight-edged marks. Never a dense `detail-dots`. The pattern is a whisper: ink at 10 to 16% of the text colour, or the metal at low strength.

## Mockups first

`business-card`, `letterhead`, `key-card` (hospitality), `shopping-bag` (fashion, beauty), `storefront-sign`, `menu` (hospitality). Paper pieces on the dark ground with long soft shadows. A screen only if the brand sells online.

## The one bold move

Choose one:

- an oversize crop of the mark across the hero, cut by the edge;
- one line of display type at 120 px or more, a quote from the brand;
- the full mark large and alone, with a whole screen of empty ground around it.

## Bans

- Rounded, bubbly or geometric display faces.
- More than one accent colour.
- Saturated gradients, glows, metallic texture images.
- Filled icons.
- Centred body text longer than two lines.
- The word "luxury" (or "exclusive", "premium") said about the brand unless the brand says it.

## Checked against

- Precise tech: no mono labels as decoration and no visible grids here; tech's tight sans display is out.
- Warm handmade: no kraft, no paper grain, no drawn textures; handmade's warm clutter would break the silence.
- Trusted authority: shares the serif, but luxury is quieter, darker and sparer; authority's ruled tables and deep institutional blues are not used here.
- Bright playful: the opposite energy. No saturation, no tilt, no rounded type.
- Natural organic: shares the calm. Luxury leans on metal and dark grounds, organic on earth tones and light grounds; organic's flowing bands are not used here.
