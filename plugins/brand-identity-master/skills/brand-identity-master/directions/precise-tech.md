# Precise tech

Clear, systematic, honest about how things work. The board looks like a well-made specification.

## When it fits

- Tech 4 to 5 on the axes, energy anywhere.
- Geometric, monoline or grid-built marks, sans wordmarks, marks made of modules.
- One strong action colour from the logo, or two that work as a pair.
- Often software, devices, data, developer tools, technical services.

## Mood words

precise, clear, fast, honest, systematic.

## Avoid

ornate, vague, nostalgic, glossy, mystical.

## Type

- Pairings: `tech-grotesk-dmsans`, `tech-plex`, `tech-syne-work`, `tech-manrope-instrument`, `play-bricolage-outfit` (for a friendlier product). Hebrew: `tech-he-plex`, `play-he-secular-heebo`.
- Display tracking slightly tight (-0.01 to -0.02em), weights 500 to 700.
- The mono face carries labels, codes and numbers, with tabular figures.
- Body 16 to 17 px with short line lengths.
- Sentence case everywhere except tiny labels.

## Colour

- A light ground, or a tinted near-black for a product that lives in dark mode. The logo decides: a dark logo gets a light ground.
- The logo's primary is the action colour: buttons, links, one highlight. One secondary at most.
- The full 50 to 950 scale strip is shown, because products use it for states (hover, disabled, surfaces).
- Contrast pairs are shown for every state colour.
- No purple-to-blue gradient unless the logo has one.

## Layout

### A Spec sheet

The 12-column grid drawn as thin rules (`proportion-grid` on the whole sheet). Every block is a labelled cell, and every value sits in a mono label. It reads like a data sheet.

### B Product first

The hero splits 6/6: the logo and one line on one side, a large `app-screen` or `website-home` mockup on the other. The system blocks follow in a compact two-column grid.

### C Modular

Equal modules with 1 px borders and the logo's own corner radius (0 to 8 px). The logo system, the palette, the type and the icons each take one module.

In every variant, the spacing is on an 8 px rhythm and every edge aligns to the grid.

Layout families: A uses the cards layout with a split hero; B the editorial layout with the name-led hero; C the cards layout with a band hero on the drawn grid.

## Pattern

`proportion-grid` (cells from the logo's bounding-box ratio), `stroke-lines` at the logo's stroke, a fine `detail-dots` field. A pattern here is structural, never decorative noise.

## Mockups first

`app-screen`, `website-home`, `social-post`, `sticker`, `business-card`, `email-signature`. Screens lead. Paper is secondary.

## The one bold move

Choose one:

- one oversize product screen, crisp, at the size of a real device;
- the mark rebuilt large on the drawn grid, showing how it is constructed;
- a single giant mono numeral or code from the system (a step of the scale, a hex code) as typography.

## Bans

- Script and handwriting faces; serif display.
- Skeuomorphic shadows, glows, neon on black.
- 3D renders, illustration clutter.
- Stock tech words ("next-gen", "seamless", "cutting-edge") anywhere.

## Checked against

- Quiet luxury: no serif display, no wide tracked capitals, no metal. Luxury's emptiness reads as missing information here.
- Warm handmade: no kraft, no drawn textures, no tilted pieces.
- Trusted authority: authority's deep institutional colour and ruled serif tables differ from tech's single action colour and mono specs.
- Bright playful: playful's tilt, heavy rounded type and saturated fields are out. Tech stays level and exact.
- Natural organic: organic's flowing forms, earth tones and soft bands are out.
