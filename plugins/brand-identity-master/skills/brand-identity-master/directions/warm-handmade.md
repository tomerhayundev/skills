# Warm handmade

Close, generous, made by people. The board feels like a counter you can lean on.

## When it fits

- Warmth 4 to 5 on the axes.
- Drawn, stamped, irregular or rounded marks; visible hand in the line.
- Warm or earthy inks: browns, oranges, tomato reds, mustard, cream.
- Often bakeries, cafes, crafts, small local shops, family businesses.

## Mood words

warm, honest, crafted, generous, local, everyday.

## Avoid

cold, sterile, corporate, glossy, rigid.

## Type

- Pairings: `warm-fraunces-nunito`, `warm-young-karla`, `warm-caveat-cabin`, `warm-lora-raleway`, `nat-dmserif-dmsans`. Hebrew: `warm-he-amatic-rubik`.
- Display has soft serifs or round terminals.
- A handwriting face appears once at most, as an accent (one word or the brand's own short line), never for body text.
- Body 17 to 18 px, friendly sans.
- Capitals only for short labels.

## Colour

- Grounds of paper and kraft: the light neutral warmed toward the logo's hue, and a kraft tone from the scale.
- Primary from the logo. The accent comes from its second ink.
- Neutrals are always warm, never grey; the near-black is a dark brown or a warm charcoal.
- Two grounds may alternate (paper and kraft) to separate blocks.

## Layout

### A Market table

A straight grid. Mockups are tilted by at most 2 degrees, as if set down on a table. Blocks sit on a paper ground with kraft bands between sections.

### B Recipe card

Stacked full-width bands, each one block, with a low-density `detail-dots` paper grain on alternate bands. Labels sit like handwritten tabs at the top left of each band.

### C Shopfront

A large hero carried by the `storefront-sign` mockup with the mark, then the system blocks below in a simple 6/6 grid.

In every variant, corners follow the logo (round marks get 8 to 16 px radii), and shadows are short and soft.

Layout families: A uses the editorial layout with the name-led hero and a collage; B the cards layout with a band hero of the mark tiled; C the editorial layout with the sign as the hero.

## Pattern

`mark-tile` from the tint version (the mark repeated like a stamp), `detail-dots` as a quiet grain, `mark-crop` on a kraft band.

## Mockups first

`takeaway-bag`, `pastry-box`, `sticker`, `product-label`, `storefront-sign`, `social-post`, `menu`. Paper and packaging lead. A screen only for a shop that sells online.

## The one bold move

Choose one:

- the mark tiled large on a full kraft band;
- one word of the brand's own, in the handwriting accent, at 100 px or more;
- the sign mockup at full width with the mark big.

## Bans

- Cool greys, blue-greys, pure white grounds.
- Hairline luxury serifs; neon.
- Visible tech grids; mono labels.
- "Artisan", "handcrafted" or "homemade" said about the brand unless the brand says it.
- Stock bakery or craft clichés as decoration (rolling pins, wheat sheaves) that are not in the logo.

## Checked against

- Quiet luxury: luxury's dark grounds, metal and silence would make this cold; no tracked capitals here.
- Precise tech: tech's grids, mono type and single action colour are out.
- Trusted authority: authority's ruled tables and deep institutional colour are too formal.
- Bright playful: shares friendliness; handmade is earthier and calmer, with no saturated fields or heavy display.
- Natural organic: shares earth tones. Handmade is about people and paper, organic about plants and calm; leafy forms not in the logo are not used here.
